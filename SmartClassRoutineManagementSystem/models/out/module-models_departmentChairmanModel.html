(inner) createChairmanToDepartmentTable() → {Promise.<void>}

Creates the DepartmentChairman table if it does not already exist. The table associates department IDs with teacher IDs, establishing a many-to-many relationship between departments and their chairpersons. The primary key for this table is a composite key consisting of department ID and teacher ID. Both fields reference the corresponding IDs in the Department and Teacher tables.
