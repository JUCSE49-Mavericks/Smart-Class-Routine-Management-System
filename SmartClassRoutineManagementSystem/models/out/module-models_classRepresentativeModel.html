(inner) createClassRepresentativeTable() → {void}

Creates the `ClassRepresentative` table if it does not exist in the database. This table includes foreign keys for `exam_year_id` (references `ExamYear`) and `student_id` (references `Student`). The `role` field is an ENUM with values 'Male' and 'Female', ensuring unique representatives for each role per exam year.
